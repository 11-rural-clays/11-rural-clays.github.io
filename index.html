LIAG Analytics Privacy Policy

LIAG Analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of the Life Is Always Good and Everything for Mortals YouTube channels. Through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API it reads those channels' own statistics and comments, and, only when the owner approves, updates the owner's own video details and posts the owner's replies to comments.

It is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other person's account. The data goes into a report on the owner's computer, and the sign-in token is kept in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is shared, sold or sent anywhere else.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
